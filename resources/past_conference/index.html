Past Conference

Completed statistics, biostatistics, bioinformatics, ML, AI, and related health research events.

2026-09-26
Table of Contents

Completed events are collected here for reference. For upcoming meetings and ongoing seminar series, see Statistics, ML & AI events.

2026

Atlanta and nearby Georgia

  • Atlanta Mathematics of Data Science Bootcamp — September 19–20 · Georgia Tech, Atlanta. Tutorials and projects on the mathematical foundations of data science and ML, for undergraduate and high-school students.
  • ACM AI Leadership Summit — August 31–September 2 · Hyatt Regency Atlanta. AI research, agentic systems, governance, and applications across academia and industry.
  • STI Prevention Conference — August 31–September 2 · InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta. Applied public-health research on sexually transmitted infections, prevention strategies, and service delivery, connecting researchers with clinical and community partners.
  • FOPAM 2026 — July 26–30 · Courtyard by Marriott Atlanta Decatur Downtown/Emory, Decatur. Process and product analytics and machine learning. The July 26–27 pre-conference workshop connected experimental design and regression with deep learning, LLMs, and AI agents.
  • Summer Institute in Statistics and Modeling in Infectious Diseases (SISMID) — July 13–24 · Emory University, Atlanta. In-person short courses in statistical and infectious-disease modeling, including ML, MCMC, and simulation-based inference; online modules ran June 22–26.
  • Atlanta Cloud + AI Conference — May 30 · FCS Innovation Academy, Alpharetta. Practitioner sessions on cloud infrastructure, AI applications, RAG, and model deployment. See the organizer’s 2026 event packet.
  • STATGEN 2026 — May 18–20 · Emory University, Atlanta. The ASA Section on Statistics in Genomics and Genetics conference, co-organized by Emory’s Department of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics, on statistical methodology for genomic and genetic data.
  • 11th Workshop on Biostatistics and Bioinformatics — May 8–10 · Georgia State University, Atlanta. Organized by Yichuan Zhao, with research talks and student posters. Yong Chen’s May 8 short course covered federated learning for collaborative health research using PDA and the MOSAiC framework.
  • CDC Epidemic Intelligence Service (EIS) Conference — April 21–24 · Atlanta. Applied epidemiology, disease investigations, and public-health data analysis. The organizer links to the 2026 agenda, presentation abstracts, and recorded sessions.
  • Atlanta AI Week 2026 — April 20–22 · Atlanta Tech Village, Buckhead. Enterprise AI, practical workshops, and startup networking.
  • Georgia State Undergraduate Research Conference (GSURC) — April 6 · Student Center East, Atlanta. Undergraduate research, scholarship, and creative projects across disciplines.
  • INFORMS Optimization Society Conference — March 20–22 · The Ritz-Carlton, Atlanta. Optimization for data science, decisions under uncertainty, and computational methods.
  • Computational Cognition Conference (CoCo Con) — March 13 · Georgia Tech, Atlanta. Computational approaches to cognition and their connections with AI.
  • Southeast Regional Clinical & Translational Science Conference — March 11–13 · Callaway Resort and Gardens, Pine Mountain, Georgia. Georgia CTSA’s regional meeting connected clinical and translational researchers. The 2026 program included medical AI implementation, clinical prediction, imaging, and LLM applications.
  • Georgia CTSA BERD Research Forum — January 30 · Georgia Tech, Atlanta. The Biostatistics, Epidemiology, and Research Design forum focused on artificial intelligence in medical and health care systems, with research presentations and graduate-student posters.

Elsewhere

  • ASA/IMS Spring Research Conference — May 26–28 · Clemson University, South Carolina. A regional option beyond Georgia for research in statistics and its applications.
  • Design and Analysis of Experiments — May 19–21 · Rutgers University, New Jersey. Research on experimental design and analysis.
